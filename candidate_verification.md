# Candidate Verification: Spitzenkandidaten / Kanzlerkandidaten BTW 2002–2021

Verification of official party Spitzenkandidaten (SK) / Kanzlerkandidaten (KK) for all federal elections relevant to this project, with citable sources for each candidate.

This file also absorbs the compact candidate-coverage overview that was previously kept separately.

## Coverage Summary at a Glance

| Year | Main coverage issue in Politbarometer |
|---|---|
| 2002 | Gabi Zimmer is absent; Gregor Gysi appears but was not the official PDS SK |
| 2009 | Renate Künast and Gregor Gysi are absent |
| 2013 | Katrin Göring-Eckardt is absent; Sahra Wagenknecht is not an official SK |
| 2017 | Katrin Göring-Eckardt, Dietmar Bartsch, Alice Weidel, and Alexander Gauland are absent |
| 2021 | Janine Wissler, Dietmar Bartsch, Alice Weidel, and Tino Chrupalla are absent; Sahra Wagenknecht appears but was not an official SK |

The detailed candidate-by-candidate verification below remains the authoritative record.

## BTW 2002

| Candidate | Party | Role | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gerhard Schröder | SPD | Kanzlerkandidat | ✅ Confirmed | Incumbent chancellor, SPD-Bundesparteitag; documented in all major BTW 2002 studies (Forschungsgruppe Wahlen, Wahlstudie BTW 2002; ZA3849/3850 codebook) |
| Edmund Stoiber | CDU/CSU | Kanzlerkandidat | ✅ Confirmed | CDU/CSU-Nominierung; Noelle-Neumann & Grünewald (2002) *Gesellschaft und Politik*; Forschungsgruppe Wahlen Wahlstudie BTW 2002 |
| Joschka Fischer | Grüne | Spitzenkandidat | ✅ Confirmed | Grünen-Bundesparteitag Dortmund 2002; documented in Zittel & Gschwend (2008) *APSR* |
| Guido Westerwelle | FDP | Spitzenkandidat | ✅ Confirmed | FDP-Bundesparteitag 2002; FDP Ergebnis 7.4%; Forschungsgruppe Wahlen BTW 2002 |
| **Gabi Zimmer** | **PDS** | **Spitzenkandidatin** | ✅ **Confirmed** | Zimmer was PDS-Vorsitzende (elected 2000) and led the PDS as Spitzenkandidatin into BTW 2002. The PDS's poor result (4.0%, excluded via 5%-Klausel) ended her leadership. **Cite: Hough, Koß & Olsen (2007), *The Left Party in Contemporary German Politics*, Palgrave Macmillan, pp. 38–42; Neugebauer & Stöss (2008) in *Parteien in Deutschland*; PDS-Parteitag Cottbus, Oct. 2001.** |

> **Note on Gregor Gysi (2002):** Gysi appears in the Politbarometer (v95) but was **not** the PDS national Spitzenkandidat. He had resigned from the PDS executive in 2002 after the "Diätenaffäre" and ran only as a Berlin direct-constituency candidate (Treptow-Köpenick), which he won. His inclusion in PB while the actual SK Zimmer is absent is a documented measurement gap.
